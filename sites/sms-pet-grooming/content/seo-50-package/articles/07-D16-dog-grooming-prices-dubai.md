## Why dog size affects the grooming price

A larger dog generally requires more:

- shampoo and coat product;
- bathing and drying time;
- workspace and handling;
- brushing and coat separation; and
- time for nails, paws and body areas.

Size alone does not explain every quote. A small dog with a dense, heavily matted coat may require more detailed work than a larger dog with a simple smooth coat.

SMS uses small, medium and large categories. Do not guess the category from breed name alone. Confirm the live weight thresholds and the individual dog’s current weight.

### 1. Coat length and density

A smooth-coated dog is normally faster to wash and dry than a long, curly or dense double-coated dog.

Curly coats require careful separation to prevent root-level mats. Double coats can hold large amounts of loose undercoat, especially during seasonal shedding.

### 2. Matting and coat condition

Tight mats are not ordinary tangles. They may pull the skin and limit the haircut that can be completed.

A groomer may recommend localised coat removal, a shorter full-body result or veterinary assessment when the matting is severe or the skin appears injured.

Do not cut close mats with scissors at home.

### 3. Haircut and style

A tidy outline trim, a sanitary trim and a complete styled haircut require different amounts of time.

A longer finished style usually requires more preparation and coat condition than a short practical trim. Reference photos help, but the dog’s coat determines what is possible.

### 4. Shedding and undercoat

A dog in a heavy shed may need additional brushing, drying and undercoat removal. Deshedding is displayed as an available SMS add-on and should be confirmed during booking.

Deshedding does not stop normal shedding. It removes loose dead coat and helps manage the amount released into the home.

### 5. Behaviour and mobility

Dogs that are fearful, reactive, elderly or uncomfortable may need a slower appointment or a reduced scope.

A full package cannot be guaranteed before the dog is assessed. Pain, wounds, severe fear or the need for medication should be discussed with a veterinarian.

### 6. Additional services

The page displays add-ons such as:

- dematting or detangling;
- deshedding;
- medicated bathing;
- teeth brushing;
- nail clipping;
- ear cleaning;
- eye-area trimming;
- anal gland expression; and
- bum or face trimming.

Not every add-on is suitable or necessary for every dog. Medical concerns should not be treated as a simple grooming upgrade.

## Mobile dog grooming versus salon price

A mobile service includes doorstep convenience. The owner does not need to transport the dog to a salon and return for collection.

A salon may use a different price structure. Compare:

- dog-size definitions;
- package inclusions;
- haircut or style work;
- dematting and deshedding charges;
- add-ons;
- appointment duration;
- transport and waiting time; and
- policies for anxious or difficult dogs.

The cheapest starting price is not automatically the best value if the package does not include the required service.
