# Ragdoll Cat Grooming: Brushing, Mat Prevention and Grooming Frequency

**Ragdolls have a moderately long, silky coat with relatively little woolly undercoat. Weekly combing is a practical starting point for many cats, with extra attention during shedding and around friction areas where tangles form.**

The coat is often easier to maintain than a dense Persian coat, but it still needs consistent care.

The Ragdoll coat is generally:

- semi-long;
- soft and silky;
- longer around the neck, body and tail;
- and less woolly than some other long-haired breeds.

This structure may reduce severe matting compared with a dense cotton-like undercoat, but tangles can still develop.

## A practical Ragdoll grooming schedule

| Task | Starting schedule |
|---|---|
| Full-body combing | About weekly |
| Friction-area checks | Several times a week |
| Heavy shedding | Increase brushing |
| Nail checks | Regularly |
| Bathing | Condition-based |
| Professional grooming | Based on mats, shedding, nails and owner ability |

Individual coat density and lifestyle matter.

## Where Ragdolls develop tangles

Check:

- behind the ears;
- armpits;
- chest;
- belly;
- groin;
- back legs;
- and the base of the tail.

A surface brush may pass over the coat while a tangle forms underneath.

## Brushing and mat prevention

Use short regular sessions rather than waiting for a long difficult appointment.

Stop if the cat becomes distressed or the coat pulls tightly on the skin.

Do not use scissors on mats.

For skin-tight or widespread mats, read [Matted Cat Fur](https://smspetgrooming.ae/matted-cat-fur-grooming/).

Ragdolls can release noticeable loose hair.

Brushing and professional deshedding may reduce loose coat and hair ingestion, but they cannot stop normal shedding.

A sudden increase in coat loss, baldness, itching or overgrooming needs veterinary assessment.

## Do Ragdolls need baths?

Most healthy cats do not need frequent routine baths.

Bathing may be useful for:

- contamination;
- an oily coat;
- long-coat maintenance;
- or veterinarian-directed care.

Complete drying and coat separation are important.

Read [Should Cats Be Bathed?](https://smspetgrooming.ae/should-cats-be-bathed/) for the broader decision framework.

## Should a Ragdoll be shaved or given a lion cut?

Routine shaving is not required only because the coat is long or the Dubai weather is hot.

A shorter maintained style may be discussed for:

- recurring mats;
- hygiene;
- owner maintenance limits;
- or severe coat condition.

The cat’s skin, coat and tolerance should guide the decision.

## Frequently asked questions

### How often should a Ragdoll be groomed?

Weekly combing is a practical starting point. Increase care during shedding or when tangles appear.

### Do Ragdolls mat easily?

They may mat less than some dense long-haired breeds, but friction areas still tangle.

### Does a Ragdoll need a lion cut?

No. The style is optional and should be based on coat condition and maintenance goals.

### Can professional deshedding reduce hair?

It can reduce loose fur but does not stop the natural hair cycle.

### Should a Ragdoll be bathed monthly?

There is no universal monthly requirement. Bathing should be condition-based.
