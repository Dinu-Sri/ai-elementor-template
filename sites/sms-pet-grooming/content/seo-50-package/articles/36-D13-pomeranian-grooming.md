# Pomeranian Grooming: Coat Care, Deshedding and Trimming

**Pomeranians have a profuse double coat that needs frequent brushing and regular professional grooming. A full professional groom every four to six weeks is a practical starting point for many pet Pomeranians.**

Routine care should preserve the coat’s structure rather than relying on close shaving.

The coat has:

- a soft dense undercoat;
- longer outer guard hairs;
- a thick neck ruff;
- feathering;
- and a full tail.

The fluffy outline depends on both coat layers.

## A practical grooming schedule

| Task | Starting schedule |
|---|---|
| Brushing | At least weekly; more during shedding |
| Friction-area checks | Several times weekly |
| Bathing and drying | Condition-based within professional schedule |
| Professional groom | Often every four to six weeks |
| Nails and paws | Regular checks |
| Deshedding | Based on loose undercoat |

The individual coat and home maintenance determine the final interval.

## Brushing and mat prevention

Pay attention to:

- behind the ears;
- armpits;
- chest;
- legs;
- groin;
- and under the tail.

A surface brush can leave compacted undercoat near the skin.

Short, regular sessions are better than waiting until the coat is tangled.

## Deshedding the undercoat

Deshedding removes loose dead coat and helps maintain the fluffy outline.

It does not stop the natural shedding cycle.

Professional deshedding may be useful when:

- the undercoat is compacted;
- shedding is heavy;
- home brushing does not reach the skin;
- or complete drying is difficult.

Read [Dog Deshedding](https://smspetgrooming.ae/dog-deshedding/).

## Bathing and complete drying

A dense coat needs complete rinsing and drying.

Damp undercoat can contribute to tangles, odour or skin irritation.

Dryer temperature and intensity should be adjusted for the dog’s tolerance and health.

Common tidying may include:

- feet;
- hygiene areas;
- ears;
- feathering;
- and the outline.

A rounded tidy finish should preserve enough guard hair to maintain the coat.

The exact style should be discussed with photographs and maintenance expectations.

## Teddy-bear cuts and very short styles

“Teddy bear cut” is not one standard length.

Very short clipping can change the appearance and may affect regrowth. The owner should understand:

- how much coat will be removed;
- whether guard hair will be cut;
- and how the coat may look during regrowth.

## Why routine close shaving is different

Close shaving is not a standard shedding or cooling solution for a healthy Pomeranian double coat.

It may still be discussed for:

- severe matting;
- medical treatment;
- or an individual welfare need.

Patchy hair loss or poor regrowth requires veterinary assessment.

## Frequently asked questions

### How often should a Pomeranian be groomed?

Many pet Pomeranians benefit from professional grooming every four to six weeks.

### Should a Pomeranian be shaved?

Not routinely. Severe matting or medical need may create an exception.

### How often should the coat be brushed?

At least weekly, with more frequent care during shedding or tangling.

### Does deshedding thin the coat permanently?

No. It removes loose dead hair rather than stopping growth.

### Is a teddy-bear cut safe?

The answer depends on length, coat condition and whether guard hairs are being cut very short.
