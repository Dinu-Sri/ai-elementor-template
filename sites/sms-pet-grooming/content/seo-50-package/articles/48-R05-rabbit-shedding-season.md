# Rabbit Shedding Season: Managing Loose Fur and Coat Changes

**Rabbits shed in cycles, and some moults release much more fur than others. During heavy shedding, increase brushing and loose-fur removal because rabbits swallow hair while grooming and cannot vomit.**

Normal moult can temporarily look uneven, but persistent baldness, inflamed skin, reduced appetite or fewer droppings needs veterinary advice.

A moult may involve:

- loose tufts;
- uneven coat length;
- visible lines between old and new coat;
- temporary thin areas;
- and a large increase in fur around the enclosure.

The pattern varies by rabbit.

## How often do rabbits shed?

Many rabbits have several shedding cycles each year.

Indoor temperature and lighting can make the pattern less predictable.

Focus on the current coat rather than a strict seasonal calendar.

## How grooming should change

During heavy shedding:

- brush more frequently;
- remove loose tufts gently;
- check the complete body;
- keep long-haired areas separated;
- and arrange professional help when the coat is difficult to manage.

Do not pull coat that is still firmly attached.

## Why swallowed fur matters

Rabbits swallow fur while grooming.

Hair normally moves through the digestive system with food and normal gut movement.

The serious warning signs are not simply the presence of loose fur. They include:

- reduced appetite;
- fewer or smaller droppings;
- lethargy;
- pain;
- and abdominal discomfort.

These signs require prompt veterinary assessment.

Do not give laxatives, hairball remedies or supplements without an exotics veterinarian.

Temporary uneven areas can occur during moult.

Contact a veterinarian for:

- persistent baldness;
- inflamed skin;
- itching;
- wounds;
- crusting;
- parasites;
- or failure of the coat to regrow.

A groomer should not diagnose the cause.

## Long-haired rabbits

Angora and Lionhead-type coats need daily maintenance throughout the year.

Heavy shedding increases mat and swallowed-fur risk.

Read [Long-Haired Rabbit Grooming](https://smspetgrooming.ae/long-haired-rabbit-grooming/).

## When professional deshedding may help

Professional care may be useful when:

- loose coat is very heavy;
- the owner cannot reach all areas;
- the rabbit resists home brushing;
- long hair is matting;
- or nail and coat care can be combined.

The provider should use rabbit-appropriate handling and stop when the rabbit is distressed.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does rabbit shedding last?

The duration varies. Some moults are quick, while others progress across the body over several weeks.

### Is a bald patch normal during shedding?

It can be, but persistent or irritated baldness needs veterinary assessment.

### Can swallowed fur cause a blockage?

Hair can contribute to serious digestive problems when gut movement or food intake is abnormal. Appetite and droppings are important warning signs.

### Should a shedding rabbit be bathed?

No. Routine bathing is discouraged.

### How often should I brush during a heavy moult?

Increase the frequency according to the amount of loose coat, sometimes to daily care.
