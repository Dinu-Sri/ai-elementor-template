# Long-Haired Rabbit Grooming: Angora and Lionhead Coat Care

**Long-haired rabbits need daily grooming because their coats mat quickly and collect loose fur. Angora-type wool coats can require extensive maintenance, while Lionheads often need focused care around the mane and longer body areas.**

A long coat should be kept only when the household can maintain it consistently.

| Rabbit type | Typical coat concern |
|---|---|
| Angora | Long wool over much of the body |
| Lionhead | Longer mane with variable body coat |
| Other long-haired crosses | Uneven long areas and friction mats |

Individual rabbits can differ from breed descriptions.

## Daily coat-care schedule

Daily checks should cover:

- chin;
- neck;
- chest;
- armpits;
- belly;
- legs;
- feet;
- groin;
- and hindquarters.

Loose fur and early tangles should be addressed before they become skin-tight mats.

## Why long coats mat quickly

Matting develops from:

- friction;
- loose hair;
- moisture;
- debris;
- soiling;
- and incomplete coat separation.

A neat surface can hide a compacted layer near the skin.

## High-risk areas

Mats form quickly around:

- the chin and chest;
- behind front legs;
- feet;
- hind legs;
- and the hygiene area.

Soiling around the hindquarters may indicate a medical or mobility problem.

Some owners work with an experienced professional to keep a long-haired rabbit shorter.

A maintained shorter coat can:

- reduce mat burden;
- simplify hygiene;
- reduce loose-fur ingestion;
- and make daily care more realistic.

The service should be performed by someone experienced with thin rabbit skin.

## Severe or felted mats

Do not use scissors.

A severe felted coat may require:

- experienced clipping;
- a rabbit-savvy veterinarian;
- or sedation under veterinary control.

Read [Matted Rabbit Fur](https://smspetgrooming.ae/matted-rabbit-fur/).

## Shedding and swallowed fur

Long-haired rabbits swallow loose fur while grooming.

Increase coat care during heavy shedding and monitor appetite and droppings.

Read the [rabbit shedding guide](https://smspetgrooming.ae/rabbit-shedding-season/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do Angora rabbits need daily grooming?

Yes. Daily coat care is generally required.

### How often should a Lionhead be groomed?

Frequent checks are needed, especially around the mane and longer areas. Many need daily or near-daily attention.

### Should a long-haired rabbit be shaved?

A shorter maintained coat may be appropriate, but severe mats and medical cases need specialist assessment.

### Can I cut mats with scissors?

No. Rabbit skin is thin and easily injured.

### Is monthly professional grooming enough?

Not without daily home care.
