## What does Cat Basic Grooming cover?

The Basic package is designed for routine maintenance. The service page shows nail and paw care, face, ear and eye cleaning, washing, drying and coat-care elements as part of the package presentation.

A Basic appointment is generally the more suitable starting point when the cat’s coat is in manageable condition and the owner mainly needs routine cleaning, brushing and hygiene support. It may also suit a short-haired cat that does not need a significant style change.

Basic does not mean that every cat receives an identical session. A nervous cat, a senior cat or a cat with painful tangles may need the appointment scope adjusted. The groomer should always prioritise safe, necessary care over trying to complete every optional step.

## What does Cat Full Grooming add?

Full Grooming is the broader package. It is intended for cats that need more extensive coat work, including trimming or shaving where appropriate.

Owners may consider the Full option when:

- the coat needs a significant length or style change;
- long fur requires a more complete trim;
- hygiene areas need broader clipping;
- the cat has coat problems that cannot be addressed through routine brushing alone; or
- the desired result involves a maintained haircut rather than basic coat care.

The final style cannot always be guaranteed before the coat is assessed. Severe matting, sensitive skin or limited tolerance may require a shorter, welfare-first result.

## Six factors that can change a cat-grooming quote

### 1. The selected package

Basic and Full Grooming have different starting prices because they are designed for different amounts of work. Selecting the correct package at the beginning reduces confusion when the groomer arrives.

### 2. Coat length and density

A short, smooth coat is normally faster to wash, dry and brush than a long, dense coat. Persian, Maine Coon and other long-haired cats can require more time because the coat must be checked in layers and dried completely.

### 3. Matting and tangles

A small, early tangle is different from a tight mat attached close to the skin. Extensive matting may require careful clipping rather than prolonged combing. This can change the required service, appointment time and finished appearance.

Owners should not cut close mats with scissors. Cat skin is thin and can be pulled into the mat. A professional assessment is safer when the mat is tight, widespread or causing discomfort.

### 4. Haircut or shaving requirements

A sanitary trim, a limited tidy-up and a full-body shave are not the same amount of work. The groomer must consider coat condition, the requested style, skin safety and how well the cat tolerates clippers.

### 5. Behaviour and appointment tolerance

A calm cat that accepts brushing, drying and paw handling may complete the planned service efficiently. A frightened or defensive cat may need a slower pace, breaks or a reduced scope. This does not mean the cat is “bad.” It means the appointment must be adapted to the individual animal.

A high-risk cat may need veterinary support rather than a normal grooming appointment. Medication or sedation decisions must come from a veterinarian.

### 6. Optional add-ons

The service page displays additional options such as dematting or detangling, deshedding, medicated bathing, teeth brushing, eye-area trimming, nail clipping and ear cleaning. Availability and suitability should be confirmed for the individual cat.

A medicated bath should not be selected simply because the cat is itchy. Skin problems, persistent odour, wounds or unusual coat loss should first be assessed by a veterinarian.

A mobile quote should be judged by the complete service model, not only by comparing one number with a salon price.

Mobile grooming removes the need for the owner to transport the cat to a salon. This can be valuable for cats that dislike car travel, owners managing several pets and households where travel and waiting time are difficult.

A salon may sometimes offer a lower starting price or have a larger fixed facility. However, the total comparison should include:

- package inclusions;
- transport and waiting time;
- whether the cat remains around unfamiliar animals;
- coat assessment and communication;
- add-on prices;
- handling of nervous or senior cats; and
- whether the requested style is realistically achievable.

Neither format is automatically better or cheaper for every cat. The more useful question is whether the service matches the cat’s coat, behaviour and household needs.

## How to compare cat-grooming quotes fairly

Before choosing a provider, ask the same questions for every quote:

1. Is this Basic Grooming, Full Grooming or a bath-only service?
2. Which exact services are included?
3. Is trimming or shaving included?
4. Is dematting charged separately?
5. Are nail and ear services included?
6. Does the groomer come to the home?
7. Can the provider work with the cat’s coat type and behaviour history?
8. What happens if the full requested service cannot be completed safely?
9. Are there additional charges that should be confirmed before the appointment?
10. What aftercare guidance is provided?

A transparent quote should make it easy to understand what is included and which conditions may change the final recommendation.

## When should you request a custom assessment?

Discuss the cat’s condition before booking when the cat:

- has tight or widespread mats;
- is elderly or has limited mobility;
- has a known bite or scratch history;
- has reacted strongly during previous grooming;
- has wounds, skin irritation or unusual hair loss;
- needs a major coat change; or
- is taking medication or has a medical condition that affects handling.

Clear information helps the groomer recommend a suitable package and prevents the appointment from beginning with unrealistic expectations.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does cat grooming cost in Dubai?

SMS Pet Grooming currently lists Cat Basic Grooming at AED 200 and Cat Full Grooming at AED 280. Other providers may use different package structures, so compare the inclusions as well as the price.

### Is a cat haircut included in Basic Grooming?

The Basic package is primarily for routine coat and hygiene care. Broader trimming or shaving is more closely associated with Full Grooming. Confirm the exact required result before booking.

### Does matted fur cost more to groom?

It can. Tight or widespread mats require more assessment and may need careful clipping rather than routine brushing. The cat’s welfare and skin condition determine what can be completed.

### Is mobile cat grooming more expensive than a salon?

Not always. Prices depend on the provider and package. Mobile grooming also includes doorstep convenience and removes the owner’s travel to a salon, so compare the full value rather than only the starting number.

### How often should I pay for professional cat grooming?

The answer depends on coat type, age, self-grooming ability, matting and the services needed. Many owners use a four-to-six-week routine, while short-haired cats with manageable coats may need professional care less often.
