# Poodle Grooming: Coat Maintenance, Appointment Frequency and Style Choices

**Poodles have continuously growing curly hair that can mat close to the skin. Many pet Poodles are maintained in a shorter practical trim and visit a professional groomer every four to six weeks.**

Longer styles require more frequent and thorough home brushing.

The coat is:

- curly;
- continuously growing;
- low-shedding compared with many breeds;
- and able to form mats near the roots.

Low shedding does not mean low maintenance.

## A practical grooming schedule

| Task | Short pet trim | Longer coat |
|---|---|---|
| Brushing and combing | Several times weekly | Often daily |
| Bathing | Within regular grooming cycle | More frequent maintenance may be needed |
| Professional haircut | Every four to six weeks | Similar or shorter interval |
| Face, feet and hygiene | According to chosen style | Frequent maintenance |
| Nails and ears | Regular checks | Regular checks |

The individual dog’s coat and lifestyle matter.

## Preventing mats near the skin

Curly hair can appear fluffy on top while tangles form underneath.

Common areas include:

- ears;
- neck;
- armpits;
- chest;
- legs;
- belly;
- and under collars or harnesses.

A longer coat must be combed through to the skin without pulling.

## Short pet trim versus longer style

A short pet trim offers:

- easier home maintenance;
- shorter drying time;
- reduced mat risk;
- and a predictable professional schedule.

A longer style offers more shaping options but requires more work between visits.

Choose a style based on realistic maintenance, not only appearance.

## Toy, Miniature and Standard Poodles

The three varieties differ significantly in body size.

SMS package selection should be based on the individual dog’s current weight rather than variety name alone.

Provide:

- weight;
- shoulder size;
- coat length;
- and current matting.

## What if the coat is already matted?

Limited tangles may be manageable.

Skin-tight or widespread mats may require a shorter haircut than the owner requested.

Read [Dog Dematting vs Shaving](https://smspetgrooming.ae/dog-dematting-vs-shaving/).

Do not use scissors on tight mats.

## Frequently asked questions

### How often should a Poodle go to a groomer?

Every four to six weeks is common for a maintained pet trim.

### Does a Poodle need daily brushing?

A long coat often does. A short trim still requires regular brushing.

### Do Poodles shed?

They release less loose hair than many breeds, but the coat still grows and mats.

### What is a puppy cut?

It is a practical short style, not one exact length.

### Can a matted Poodle keep a long haircut?

Not always. Safe mat removal may require a shorter result.
